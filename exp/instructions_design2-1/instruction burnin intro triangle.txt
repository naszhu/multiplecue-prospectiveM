RE-ACCLIMATION BLOCK

Before today's session, you will complete a short block of practice trials to help you get back into the rhythm of the task after your break.

The task in this block is exactly the same as in your last session.

ONGOING TASK: Choose the circle with the highest reward digit and press the matching color key. The color-key mapping will be shown on the screen during this block (colored squares below the circles).

SPECIAL TASK: On some trials, the small circle below the reward digit in one circle will be replaced by a TRIANGLE (the circle keeps its normal color and reward digit). When you see a TRIANGLE, press C with your LEFT MIDDLE FINGER instead of a right-hand color key.

These trials are for re-acclimation only and are not counted as part of the experimental data. However, any points you earn will still be added to your accumulated reward and will count toward your final payment.

This block contains 20 trials.
