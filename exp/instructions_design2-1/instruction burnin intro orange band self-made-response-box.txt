RE-ACCLIMATION BLOCK

Before today's session, you will complete a short block of practice trials to help you get back into the rhythm of the task after your break.

The task in this block is exactly the same as in your last session.

Keep your right hand on the right half of the response box and your left hand on the left half, with all five fingers of each hand resting lightly on the keys. Do not lift or move your fingers from the keys.

ONGOING TASK: Choose the circle with the highest reward digit using your right index, middle, and ring fingers. The color-finger mapping will be shown on the screen during this block (colored squares below the circles).

SPECIAL TASK: On some trials, one circle will show an ORANGE BAND between its outer color and white center (outer color and white center stay the same). When you see an ORANGE BAND, press the key under your LEFT INDEX FINGER instead of a right-hand color key.

These trials are for re-acclimation only and are not counted as part of the experimental data. However, any points you earn will still be added to your accumulated reward and will count toward your final payment.
