PRACTICE BLOCK

You have now finished the baseline sessions. The sessions from here on are different: in addition to choosing the highest-reward circle, there is sometimes a second response you must make.

You will first practice this in a short block before the main part of today's session.

On some trials, one circle will have a DASHED WHITE OUTLINE instead of the normal solid border. When you see it, press C with your left middle finger instead of the color key.

Otherwise, choose the circle with the highest reward digit using:

  Red = N     Blue = J     Green = K

During this practice block, the color-key mapping will NOT be shown on the screen. Use what you have learned.

When this practice block ends, you will see the full instructions for today's session before the main blocks begin.
