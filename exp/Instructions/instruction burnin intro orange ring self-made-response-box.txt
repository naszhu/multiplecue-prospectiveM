RE-ACCLIMATION BLOCK

Before today's session, you will complete a short block of practice trials to help you get back into the rhythm of the task after your break.

The task is the same as in your last session. In your last session, you chose the circle with the highest reward digit with your right hand and also watched for a special task: when you saw an ORANGE RING on a circle's border, you pressed the key under your LEFT INDEX FINGER instead of a right-hand color key.

This re-acclimation block contains only the color-choice trials from that task. There are no special-task trials here.

Keep your right hand on the right half of the response box, with all five fingers resting lightly on the keys. Choose the circle with the highest reward digit using your right index, middle, and ring fingers. The color-key mapping will be shown on the screen during this block (colored squares below the circles).

These trials are for re-acclimation only and are not counted as part of the experimental data. However, any points you earn will still be added to your accumulated reward and will count toward your final payment.

This block contains 20 trials.
