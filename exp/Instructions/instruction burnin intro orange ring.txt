RE-ACCLIMATION BLOCK

Before today's session, you will complete a short block of practice trials to help you get back into the rhythm of the task after your break.

The task is the same as in your last session. In your last session, you chose the circle with the highest reward digit and also watched for a special task: when you saw an ORANGE RING on a circle's border, you pressed C with your LEFT MIDDLE FINGER instead of a right-hand color key.

This re-acclimation block contains only the color-choice trials from that task. There are no special-task trials here.

Choose the circle with the highest reward digit and press the matching color key. The color-key mapping will be shown on the screen during this block (colored squares below the circles).

These trials are for re-acclimation only and are not counted as part of the experimental data. However, any points you earn will still be added to your accumulated reward and will count toward your final payment.

This block contains 20 trials.
