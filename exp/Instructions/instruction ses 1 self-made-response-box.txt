PRACTICE 1

In the following, you will see a fixation cross in the center of the screen. Please always fixate this point when you start a trial.

Upon pressing *space*, a colored circle with a digit from 1 to 3 will appear in the center of the screen.

__________

Your task is to report the color of the circle by pressing one of three keys on the response box.

Place your right hand on the right half of the response box, with all five fingers resting lightly on the keys. Keep your right hand in this position throughout the session — do not lift or move your fingers from the keys.

You will respond using three fingers on your right hand: your index finger, your middle finger, and your ring finger.

The colors for your index finger, middle finger, and ring finger are {C_INDEX}, {C_MIDDLE}, and {C_RING}. This color-finger mapping will never change across the whole experiment. The mapping is also indicated by the colored squares shown below the circle.

Also place your left hand on the left half of the response box, with all five fingers resting lightly on the keys. Keep your left hand in this position as well — do not lift or move your fingers from the keys.

__________

You will earn points by reporting the color of the circle correctly while also responding as quickly as possible.

The digit 1 allows you to earn 1 point, and so on until a maximum reward of 3 points per trial.

Your collected points will be translated into a monetary reward (0.05 DKK per point). After each trial, you will see how many points you have earned in that trial relative to what you could have earned, along with the amount of money in DKK that you have obtained.

__________

Remember to fixate the cross at the center at the start of each trial.

Press *space* to begin.
